Mythology:

Waxing gibbous moon mythology:
It represents a powerful, high energy phase of growth.
This phase represents determination and passion to oversome obstacles and achieve dreams.

Waning gibbous moon mythology:
Symbolizes a time of reflecion, letting go and gratitude.
In greek mythology associated with Demeter the goddess of agriculture and harvest.
This signifies a time to gather the fruits of ones labour.
Considered an ideal time to release burdens and prepare for new beginnigs.

Third quarter moon mythology:
Represents closing, completion and cleansing.
Symbolizes a time for letting go and clearing space.
Considered ideal for releasing old patterns and preparing for the new moon.
Often associated with both death and harvest.

Waning crescent moon mythology:
Represents rest, healing and letting go.
Preparation for the next moon cycle ahead.

Waxing crescent moon mythology:
Considered a time to take action towards plans made during the new moon.
A time for growth, intention and hope.
Associated with feminine energy, intuition and renewal.
As well as with youth, potential and fertility.

First quarter moon mythology:
Considered a energetic, independent and active time.
A time that highlights obstacles.
Therefore a time to re-evaluate plans and strengthen commitments.

New moon mythology:
Signifies transition, rest and renewal.
A quiet period to prepare for the growth that will come with the waxing moon.

Full moon mythology:
Symbolizes peak energy, fertility and transformation.
Associated with maddness and supernatural activity.
This is the origin of werewolf myths and even words like lunatic.
A time for culmination, harvest and celebration.
