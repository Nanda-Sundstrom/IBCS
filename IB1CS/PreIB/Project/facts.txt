Facts:

Waxing gibbous moon facts:
During this phase, the moon is waxing and on its way to become a full moon.
Therefore, it goes from 51% to 99% illuminated.
The waxing gibbous moon lasts for roughly 7 days.

Waning gibbous moon facts:
During this phase, the moon is waning and on its way to become a third quarter moon.
Therefore, it goes from 99% to 51% illuminated.
The waning gibbous moon lasts for roughly 7 days.

Third quarter moon facts:
Also called the last quarter moon.
During this phase, the moon is three quarter through its orbit and on its way to become a waning crescent moon.
It is 50% illuminated for only a few hours, but may appear that way for about 2 days.

Waning crescent moon facts:
This is the final phase of the lunar cycle.
Therefore, at this phase the moon is on its way to become a new moon.
It goes from 49% to 1% illuminated.
It lasts for about 7 days.

Waxing crescent moon facts:
During this phase, the moon is on its way to become a first quarter moon.
It goes from 1% to 49% illuminated.
It lasts for about 7 days.

First quarter moon facts:
During this phase the moon is on its way to become a waxing gibbous moon.
The moon is 50% illuminated.
It lasts for about 7 days.

New moon facts:
During this phase the moon is 0% illuminated.
Therefore, it appears invisible.
However, it is visible during solar eclipses.
In 2026 there are 2 solar eclipses: on 17/02 and 28/08.

Full moon facts:
During this phase, the moon is 100% illuminated.
The full moon is in sync with the sun.
Therefore, it sets and rises at the same time (6pm and 6am).
It is generally at its highest point at midnight.
During full moons, people tend to sleep about 20 min less than usual.
